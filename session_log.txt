Q: How large do Ragdoll cats get
Article: Ragdoll
A: Ragdoll cats are known for their large and weighty morphology. Adult male Ragdolls typically weigh between 15-20 pounds, while adult females usually weigh between 10-15 pounds. They have a sturdy and muscular build, with a semi-long and silky soft coat.
